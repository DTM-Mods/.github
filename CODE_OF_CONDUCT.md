# Code of conduct

DTM Mods is a small group of players who make Farming Simulator mods for fun. In our repositories,
issues, pull requests and on our Discord:

- **Be friendly and patient.** Many people here are new to modding or to GitHub.
- **Keep it about the mods.** Criticise the work, not the person.
- **No harassment, insults, slurs or spam,** and no posting other people's private details.
- **Respect other modders' work.** Credit what you use, and don't upload other people's mods or paid
  content without permission.

The admins (Fennell and PropaineX) may edit or remove posts and block people who don't follow this.
To report something, message an admin on the [DTM Mods Discord](https://discord.gg/2GTU9hAa3Q).
